Project Instructions – EasyThreed K9

### Printer Context

- Target printer: EasyThreed K9

- Official build volume: 100 × 100 × 100 mm

- Practical continuous print limit: ~2 hours (due to weak 24 W PSU and reliability)

- Max recommended speed: 40 mm/s

- No heated bed

- 0.4 mm nozzle, primarily PLA

- Workflow: OpenSCAD → STL → Ultimaker Cura (Extra Fast + custom K9 profile) → TF card → One-key print

### Current Goal

Design and print good quality objects for real-world dimensional accuracy of the K9.

### Design Rules for this Project

- Always keep print time under ~2–2.5 hours when possible

- Prefer open-top designs and thin walls for speed

- Leave at least 1 mm margin from the absolute 100 mm build limits

- Provide clean, ready-to-copy OpenSCAD code

- After any model change, give recommended Cura settings for the K9 (layer height, walls, speed, etc.)

### When the user reports print results

- Help interpret measured dimensions vs designed dimensions

- Suggest improvements or next test models based on accuracy findings

### General Behavior

- Stay focused on the K9’s limitations

- Prefer practical, fast-printing test pieces over large or slow models

- Keep responses concise and actionable
